## Kandydaci na drugie źródło

### Allen Brain Observatory – Visual Coding Neuropixels (źródło zdarzeń)

- Siegle, J. H., Jia, X., Durand, S., et al. (2021). Survey of spiking in the mouse
  visual system reveals functional hierarchy. *Nature*, 592, 86–92.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-03171-x
- [Strona zbioru](https://portal.brain-map.org/explore/circuits/visual-coding-neuropixels)
- Uwaga: nie mylić z wersją dwufotonową (obrazowanie wapniowe):
  [Visual Coding 2P](https://observatory.brain-map.org/visualcoding/stimulus/drifting_gratings)

### Blue Brain Cell Atlas (dane kontekstowe dla wymiaru regionu)

- Erö, C., Gewaltig, M.-O., Keller, D., & Markram, H. (2018). A Cell Atlas for the
  Mouse Brain. *Frontiers in Neuroinformatics*, 12, 84.
  https://doi.org/10.3389/fninf.2018.00084
- [Strona zbioru](https://portal.bluebrain.epfl.ch/resources/models/cell-atlas/)

  Distributed coding of choice, action and engagement across the mouse brain.
  *Nature*, 576, 266–273. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1787-x
- [Dane na Figshare](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Distributed_coding_of_choice_action_and_engagement_across_the_mouse_brain/9974357)
